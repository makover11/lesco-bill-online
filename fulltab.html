Understanding LESCO Identifiers

14-Digit Reference Number: Format 12 12345 1234567

  • First 2 digits: Batch / Sub-division code
  • Next 5 digits: Meter book & route number
  • Last 7 digits: Consumer account sequence

10-Digit Customer ID: Unique alphanumeric account ID printed right above your reference number.
