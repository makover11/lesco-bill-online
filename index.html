LESCO Online Bill Check

Are you tired of waiting for your physical electricity bill to arrive at your doorstep? Managing your utility expenses is now easier than ever. Customers of the Lahore Electric Supply Company (LESCO) can instantly view, download, and print their duplicate bills online.

Get Your Duplicate Bill Instantly

Enter your 14-digit reference number on our dedicated secure portal to view your latest electricity bill for free.

Check Your LESCO Bill Now

What Do You Need?

To check your LESCO bill online, you do not need to register for an account. All you need is your 14-digit Reference Number. You can find this number printed on any of your old electricity bills. It is usually located in the top left corner of the bill or just below the customer details box.

Step-by-Step Guide

  1. Grab an old LESCO bill and locate your 14-digit reference number.
  2. Click the green "Check Your LESCO Bill Now" button above.
  3. Enter your reference number carefully in the search box (without any spaces).
  4. Click the "Submit" or "Check Bill" button.
  5. Your latest bill will appear on the screen. You can choose to download it as a PDF or print it directly.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

Can I check my LESCO bill by name or CNIC?

No, currently LESCO only allows bill checking using the 14-digit reference number or Customer ID. Checking by name or CNIC is not supported to protect user privacy.

Why is my bill showing as "Not Found"?

This usually happens if you enter the wrong reference number. Double-check the number from an old bill. If the number is correct, your bill for the current month might not have been generated yet by the LESCO systems.

Can I pay my bill after checking it online?

Yes! Once you have your bill amount and reference number, you can pay using mobile banking apps like JazzCash, Easypaisa, NayaPay, or through your standard bank's online internet banking portal.
